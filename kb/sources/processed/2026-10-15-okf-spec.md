# Open Knowledge Format specification

- URL: https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/knowledge-catalog/blob/main/okf/SPEC.md
- Retrieved: 2026-10-15
- Publisher: Google Cloud, `knowledge-catalog` repository
- Why it matters: the file format this KB's pages follow, at v0.1

## What it says

Notes on the parts this KB relies on, not a copy. As of 2026-10-15 the spec is at v0.2.

- A concept page is UTF-8 Markdown with YAML frontmatter between `---` lines. `type` is the only required key, and its value is any short string. `title`, `description` (one sentence), `resource` (a canonical URI) and `tags` (a list) are recommended. Producers may add keys, and readers keep keys they don't know.
- Timestamps are ISO 8601 date-times with an explicit UTC offset. `timestamp` is a v0.1 field. v0.2 replaces it with `generated.at`, and readers fall back to `timestamp` when `generated` is missing.
- `index.md` is reserved. It lists a folder in headed sections of `[Title](url) - description` bullets. It has no frontmatter, except that the bundle root's may carry `okf_version`.
- `log.md` is reserved. It's a flat history under `## YYYY-MM-DD` headings, newest date first. A bold word at the start of each entry is conventional.
- Links that start with `/` resolve from the bundle root and are recommended. Plain relative links are allowed too. Readers must tolerate broken links.
- The folder layout is up to the producer. A bundle can be a folder inside a larger git repository.
- v0.1 listed sources under a `# Citations` heading in the page body. v0.2 moves them into a `sources` list in the frontmatter, with footnotes for single claims.
- A bundle conforms when every page that isn't reserved has frontmatter that parses, with a non-empty `type`, and its `index.md` and `log.md` files follow their formats.
